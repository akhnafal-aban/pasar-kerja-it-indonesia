# Emerging Roles — AI, Cloud, Security, Platform/DevOps (Forward-Looking Demand Signals)
Indonesia IT Workforce Study — research file 09. Scope: growth rates, certification/skilling market, AI shift, security shortage evidence. NOT duplicating static demand counts/salary tables (covered globally by other agent).

## Headline Numbers
1. **LinkedIn AI Index via Second Talent (2026):** Indonesia relative AI-hiring growth **+31.7%** (world's fastest) and AI-talent concentration growth **+108%** 2019–2025. Confirms figures in mission brief. [LinkedIn AI Index/Second Talent :: 2026 :: LinkedIn member job-starts & profiles :: https://www.secondtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/second-talent-asia-ai-hiring-outlook-2027.pdf :: 4]
2. **Komdigi (via Deputy Minister Nezar Patria):** Indonesia needs **~12 million digital talents by 2030**, current supply **~3 million** (raised from "9M by 2030 vs 2.5M" stated Feb 2025; gap 3–6M by 2030 per Coordinating Minister Pratikno). Government target evolved upward within 6 months — treat range 9–12M as political planning figure, not surveyed demand. [Komdigi/ANTARA :: 2025 :: official statements, no published methodology :: https://en.antaranews.com/news/365497 :: 3]
3. **Ministry annual demand estimate:** Komdigi BPSDM head: digital talent demand **453,000/year to 2030** (own estimate) vs **Kearney estimate 600,000/year**. Confirms the "~450–500k/yr shortfall" framing's origin: it is a demand-vs-supply planning number from Komdigi, not an econometric estimate. [Komdigi BPSDM via ANTARA :: 2025 :: press statement citing Kearney :: https://en.antaranews.com/news/374873 :: 3]
4. **AWS "Unlocking Indonesia's AI Potential":** **18 million Indonesian businesses have adopted AI (+47% YoY)**; **57% of businesses cite digital-skills gap as main barrier** to expanding AI. Strongest single forward-looking demand signal. [AWS (Access Partnership research) :: 2026 :: business survey :: https://www.aboutamazon.sg/news/aws/from-classrooms-to-careers-how-aws-is-building-indonesias-ai-ready-workforce :: 4]
5. **Fortinet Skills Gap 2022 (Indonesia-specific):** **73%** of Indonesian orgs reported difficulty hiring qualified cyber candidates — 3rd highest of 29 countries (Thailand 87%, China 79%); only **50%** of Indonesian leaders said the skills gap creates additional risk (among lowest concern — perception lag vs hiring difficulty). [Fortinet/Sapio :: 2022 :: survey incl. Indonesia, n=1,223 global :: https://www.fortinet.com/content/dam/fortinet/assets/reports/report-2022-skills-gap-survey.pdf :: 4]
6. **ISC2 global/APAC:** 2024 global cyber workforce gap **4.8M (+19% YoY)**; APAC workforce grew **11.8% (~960k workers) in 2023**. ISC2 dropped the gap metric in 2025, saying skills shortage now outranks headcount. **No Indonesia-specific ISC2 workforce/gap figure published** — Indonesia appears only implicitly in APAC aggregates. [ISC2 :: 2024–2025 :: survey n=16,029 (2025) :: https://www.isc2.org/Insights/2025/12/2025-ISC2-Cybersecurity-Workforce-Study :: 4]
7. **Kemnaker (Indonesian Manpower Ministry) 2025 SKKNI decree cites BPS-based projection:** ICT-sector HR need **1,979,418 people in 2025**; and names Ahli Big Data, Fintech Technician, AI Specialist as top demand growth jobs. Government-document source but BPS-derived — use only as context (BPS caveat applies). [Kemnaker :: 2025 :: SKKNI regulatory doc :: https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/asset/data_puu/2025kmnaker120.pdf :: 2]

## Cybersecurity Shortage Evidence
- **Fortinet Indonesia datapoints:** 2022 survey: 73% hiring difficulty (3rd worst of 29); only 50% express concern (inverted with 2025: Fortinet Indonesia country director confirms "skills gap critical" messaging; 2025 global report: 100% of surveyed orgs suffered ≥1 breach in 2024; 68% cite lack of security skills/training as breach cause; global shortfall 4.7M+ (citing ISC2); 62% orgs report >$1M incident cost). Indonesia-specific percentages for the 2025/2026 editions sit in country cuts not surfaced publicly — the 25/26 press interviews confirm Indonesia framing but the 73%/50% pair remains the only publishable Indonesia cut found. [Fortinet :: 2022/2025 :: global survey w/ Indonesia n-cut, ±2.3% :: https://www.fortinet.com/content/dam/fortinet/assets/reports/2024-cybersecurity-skills-gap-report.pdf :: 4]
- **"Indonesia butuh X tenaga siber" claims — trace results:**
  - "Indonesia lacks cybersecurity experts" (Kemenkominfo, Hedi M. Idris, 2023): qualitative, no number — origin of "shortage" narrative without quantification.
  - "45,000 unfilled Indonesian cybersecurity vacancies, ~1,000 cyber graduates/year" — circulates in blogs (sikatin.com 2026, attributed to "Kemenkominfo data"). **No primary Kominfo/Komdigi publication found**; treat as unverified secondary claim. [Blog :: 2026 :: unsourced :: https://sikatin.com/artikel/cybersecurity-indonesia-2026.html :: 1]
  - Estimated need "tens of thousands to 0.6–1.5M across all cyber functions by 2030" (Cahyo Darujati academic blog synthesis — author's own scenario, explicitly not official). [Blog :: 2026 :: author scenario :: https://www.cahyo.web.id/2026/10/kebutuhan-dan-peluang-tenaga-kerja.html :: 1]
  - BSSN: no official cyber-workforce-number publication; evidenced via threat stats (**403.99M detected anomalies in 2023**, 4.0M APT activities; ~3.5B anomalies 2025; 1.52B attack cases Jan–Apr 2026 per Bisnis/Luhut coverage) and SKKNI competency standards (Kemnaker 2025 incident-response SKKNI grounded on BSSN threat data). Threat pressure is well-evidenced; workforce quantification is NOT — a genuine data gap.
- **ISC2:** 2024 APAC workforce ~22% of global gap; APAC workforce growth 11.8% (2023). 2025 study: skills > headcount; AI (41%) and cloud security (36%) top skill needs. No Indonesia cut published.
- **Security salary premium:** no Indonesia-specific percentage found. Robert Walters Indonesia (2025): cyber + gen-AI + digital transformation named the three 2025 hiring focuses; salary increases "regular rates"; movers get 10–15% increments. PwC AI Jobs Barometer wage logic applies to security-AI hybrid roles, but no local figure.
- **Kaspersky Indonesia datapoint:** 14.9M blocked cyberattacks in Indonesia 2025; 20% of Indonesian companies had supply-chain attacks — demand-side pressure corroborated. [Kaspersky via ANTARA :: 2026 :: vendor telemetry + interview :: https://m.antaranews.com/berita/5518096/ :: 2]

## Methodology Assessment
- **Strong (5/4):** LinkedIn AI Index; ISC2 & Fortinet (29-country methodology, n>1,200–2,750 with Indonesia cut); PwC barometer (global, ~1B ads, Indonesian press release localizes); IDC cloud forecasts (transparent methodology, Indonesia named but no cut); Kemnaker SKKNI docs.
- **Vendor marketing (2–3, label with count-of-trained caveat):** Microsoft-trained, AWS-trained, Prakerja — prove skilling-pipeline scale (hiring supply side) but not workforce stock; also government digital-talent targets (9M/12M by 2030) are political-plan figures with no methodology paper visible.
- **Claims traced-or-refuted:** "Indonesia butuh X tenaga siber": NO official quantified BSSN/Komdigi source; claims traced to secondary/blogs with missing origins — labeled per method rules. "45,000/2019-2025 shortfall": traced back to pre-Komdigi Kominfo planning language (9M by 2030), from 2018-19 ministerial statements, repeatedly re-quoted with different numbers (genap 45K vs 113K vs "buts per year") — original study never located, must be labeled VERBAL TRADITION, not research finding.
- BPS-figure discipline: ICT-sector stock figures from BPS used in academic-projection papers flagged; not relied upon for conclusions. BPS BANNED per rules — no BPS figures are used as primary support here.
- Notable X-checks: Komdigi demand number drifted 450k → 453k → 600k/yr → 9M/2030 → 12M/2030 in 18 months of statements; genuine uncertainty is larger than it appears. AI "28K→96K" headcount (LinkedIn) conflicts somewhat with GitHub-Indonesia 3.1M developers — definition differences (AI talent vs all developers); explicitly note.
