# Indonesia IT Workforce Study — Corporate Landscape (Who Employs IT Workers)

Research agent: 04-corporate-landscape. Date: 2026-11-09.
Scope: tech-native companies, digital operations, IT services/outsourcing, GCCs, non-tech enterprises with IT teams, team composition.

## Methodology Assessment

1. **Audited annual reports (confidence 5)** delivered the cleanest totals: Bukalapak 1,815 (2022) / 1,538 (2023); Blibli Tiket 3,609 (2022); Telkomsel 6,455 (2023); IOH 4,041 (2023); XL 1,575 (2021); BCA ~27,000 + ~4,000 hires "mainly IT" (2023). Weakness: none break out tech vs non-tech staff except BCA's qualitative note and BNI's division table.
2. **Company layoff statements** (confidence 4) give deducible bases: GoTo 1,300=12% → ~10.8-11K; Ajaib 67=8% → ~840; Shipper 65=8%; Sirclo 160=8% (~2,000 base, corroborated by Bloomberg).
3. **Revelio Labs profile-based headcounts** (confidence 2-3) are the only source for private startups (GoTo post-2022, Traveloka, Shipper, Ajaib, Metrodata, Telkomsigma, Bank Jago). They systematically differ from payroll figures (Telkomsigma: Revelio ~6K vs LinkedIn ~2.7K vs Wikipedia 1.75K in 2018) — use as trend, not level.
4. **LinkedIn/LeadIQ/Tracxn/GetLatka** (confidence 2): profile counts (e.g., Flip 667, tiket 2,179) — coverage artifacts.
5. **Verification failures to flag**: (a) "Bank Mandiri hired 3,000 tech engineers" — NOT FOUND; actual program: MDA → ODP-IT pipeline, ~600 bootcamp grads per batch. (b) "PT Global Digital Niaga = software house" — wrong, it's Blibli. (c) "ABSI" association — unverified. (d) EPAM Indonesia — unverified. (e) Netflix/Amazon engineering hubs — unverified. (f) eFishery headcounts unreliable due to admitted fraud.
6. Definition caution: "startup" counts range <1,000 to 2,390 depending on source year and activity criteria; much IT employment sits in non-tech firms and government, which public startup-ecosystem reports miss.
7. BPS: not used (per rules).
