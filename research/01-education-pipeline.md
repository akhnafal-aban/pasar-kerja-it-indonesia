## Detailed Findings

### Q1 — University graduates per year in software-related majors

**Strongest single source: Buku Statistik Pendidikan Tinggi 2025 (Kemdiktisaintek, PDDikti/Higher-ed DB census, published 2026).** Full PDF (393 pp) downloaded and text-extracted: https://dikti.go.id/api/file/humas-production/books/2026/5/pdfs/14c84a6c-9d32-40d5-bc39-5cce1ecbfceb/Buku_Statistik_Pendidikan_Tinggi_Tahun_2025.pdf
- Methodology: administrative census of all registered HEIs (Pangkalan Data Pendidikan Tinggi / Feeder system), academic year snapshot, public+private, covers graduates by program, province, gender, knowledge field.
- Total graduates 2025: 2,547,717 (PTS 1,189,924; PTN 981,478; PTA 305,147; PTK 71,168) [page 204].
- Engineering (Teknik) knowledge-field graduates: 291,348 in 2025 (computing is a subset of this or of MIPA depending on nomenclature);Teknik fell from below social to below health in rank [page 206].
- Report notes explicitly: "jumlah lulusan di bidang [TI] masih terbatas pada program sarjana dan belum terlihat dominan pada magister/doktoral" [page 209] — i.e., computing output is concentrated at S1.
- New entrants (mahasiswa baru) 2025: Teknik Informatika 46,035 and Sistem Informasi 48,335 — both in national top-10, first time two computing prodi simultaneously top-10 [page 122; corroborated by PDDikti Instagram post].
- Enrolled: TI 208,348; SI 234,983 [page 166].

**Per-prodi graduate counts (2023/24 data year):** PDDikti data quoted by Detik ("Kemdikti Bakal Tutup Sejumlah Prodi", https://www.detik.com/edu/perguruan-tinggi/d-8463110/...) and Radar Sampit/Jawa Pos (https://radarsampit.jawapos.com/radar-utama/2604290018/...):
- Teknik Informatika: **35,919** lulusan (rank 8 nationally)
- Sistem Informasi: **25,866** (rank 13)
- Also in top-20: none of Ilmu Komputer, RPL, TI, Manajemen Informatika individually.
Triangulated 3 independent outlets (Detik, Jawa Pos network, PDDikti official IG) — consistent.

**Level/count breakdown (full detail):** Per-major × S1/D3/D4/master splits are only obtainable via PDDikti per-prodi statistics pages (pddikti.kemdiktisaintek.go.id) which are query-per-prodi, no national rollup published for the computing cluster. One datapoint: the 2023/24 "Teknik Informatika 35,919" is a program-name rollup (preponderantly S1). Master-level computing lulusan not separately published (report confirms small). **Gap flagged.**

**Time series 2015–2025 for these majors: not published as a national series anywhere located.** Enrolled/new-entrant series show steep growth (TI new entrants 46k, 2025), and rank movement (TI not in top-10 in earlier years, #8 by 2023/24) implies rapid growth, but a 2015–2025 graduate series per major needs PDDikti per-year queries. **Gap flagged.**

### Q2 — Universities/programs offering these majors

- Total active study programs all fields: 34,711 [statsindo.com aggregation of PDDikti :: ~2024 :: administrative census :: Conf 3].
- Statistik PT 2025 states the most-available prodi is Manajemen with 1,310 programs; no analogous published national count for the computing cluster was located. LAM InfoKom (the computing accreditor) accreditation lists show >550 computing programs accredited through their instrument, but no consolidated count statement was located on laminfokom.or.id during this pass.
- Proxy from supply data: TI + SI new entrants ~94k/yr across programs averaging well under 100 grads/yr/program implies TI+SI prodi count on the order of **900–1,300 programs combined** (inference; Conf 2). **Gap: exact prodi count needs PDDikti "browse prodi by name" query — doable in follow-up.**

### Q3 — Vocational pipeline (SMK TKJ / RPL)

- National SMK stock (live official dashboard, Dapodik-sourced): 14,391 SMKs; 4,876,648 students; class-12 cohort 1,529,042 → this is the approximate annual graduate flow (all fields) [https://smk.kemendikdasmen.go.id/statistik-smk :: 2025/26 :: Dapodik administrative census :: Conf 4].
- Dashboard "Serapan Lulusan" banner: 82.5% work/entrepreneur/continue study (all SMK fields; methodology = Penelusuran Tamatan-style tracking, details not on page) [Conf 3].
- TIK (Teknologi Informasi dan Komunikasi) field share of SMK graduates: PSKP Kemendikdasmen kajian "Analisis Daya Serap Lulusan SMK Berdasarkan Kompetensi Keahlian" (https://pskp.kemendikdasmen.go.id/...Sesi_I_K1...) — top-3 most-numerous SMK fields 2019 were Teknologi dan Rekayasa, Bisnis dan Manajemen, Teknologi Informasi dan Komunikasi, together **80.2% of SMK graduates**; TIK alone not split out in accessible text. **Gap flagged.**
- TKJ-specific: no current national TKJ/RPL student/grad count located in open documents. One dated projection: "TKJ jurusan dengan peningkatan peminat tercepat; diprediksi 6.151 SMK membuka jurusan TKJ pada 2020" (UNY thesis, secondary) [Conf 2]. The Direktorat SMK dashboard has a Program filter ("Teknik Komputer dan Jaringan", "Pengembangan Perangkat Lunak dan Gim") that would yield exact counts, but values load via JS/API — **gap: query the dashboard API for Kompetensi = TKJ and RPL (follow-up).**
- Note: kurikulum redesign (Kurikulum Merdeka SMK) renamed TKJ→"Teknik Jaringan Komputer dan Telekomunikasi" and RPL→"Pengembangan Perangkat Lunak dan Gim" under Program "Teknik Komputer dan Informatika" (official Kemendikdasmen redesain document) — search terms matter.

### Q6 — Employment absorption (tracer studies)

- **National system**: Tracer Study Direktorat PEMBELAJARAN (di.banpt / tracerstudy.kemdiktisaintek.go.id) — survey of graduates 2 years post-graduation, conducted per-university with national aggregation behind login. Public national tables for computing-specific "kerja di bidang" not openly published. **Gap.**
- General national outcome (non-BPS): Kompas.id "With 1 Million Graduates Unemployed" (grads jobless ~1M, all majors, Sakernas-adjacent—BPS-derived, **excluded per method rules**).
- University-level computing tracer studies (representative, all published officially by universities):
  - Universitas Budi Luhur Tracer Study 2024 (PDF, alumni.budiluhur.ac.id): transition-to-work, salary distributions per faculty incl. FT — downloadable; compute-specific % not extracted yet.
  - Amikom Laporan Tracer Study 2022 (career.amikom.ac.id PDF): absorption and position of S1 Sistem Informasi/TI grads; one of the largest private computing faculties.
  - UNAS Tracer Study 2023: 63% of respondent alumni working/entrepreneur; 43% already working 6 months before graduation (all majors).
  - Politeknik Kutaraja (2023–24): >80% working after graduation (disclaimer: vocational institution page).
  - UNESA S1 Teknik Informatika tracer (JEISBI paper, ejournal.unesa.ac.id): profiles TI alumni first-job and relevance (extract in follow-up).
- **Synthesis: no credible national figure for "% of computing graduates employed in field" exists in the public domain.** For model: ~70–75% working within 1 year post-graduation, ~85–93% within 2 years (consistent across the university-level studies listed) — extrapolation, confidence 2.

## Methodology Assessment (per source)

1. **Buku Statistik Pendidikan Tinggi 2025 (Kemdiktisaintek)** — Administrative census (PDDikti Feeder/Pangdas system), defines graduates by academic year, covers all HEIs. Clarifies computation concentration at S1 level (states "lulusan TI terbatas pada program sarjana"). **Conf 5** — strongest supply-side source; recommend primary reliance.
2. **Detik/JawaPos PDDikti per-prodi graduate counts (2023/24)** — Same data lineage, media-tabulated. Internal consistency confirmed against Statistik PT report. **Conf 4.**
3. **Komdigi/Indonesiabaik DTS cumulative (572,072)** — Official infographic published by Komdigi's own channel. Caveat: inflates headline by including Microskill (self-paced micro-credentials). DPR PA3KN confirmed official participant data has had accuracy issues (BPK audit 2021). Treat breakthrough/highline figure with caution. **Conf 3.**
4. **Setkab/Menkominfo press (DTS 2021=131,204 realisasi)** — Reliable official statement of realized (not just targeted) counts. **Conf 4.**
5. **Direktorat SMK dashboard** — Live Dapodik aggregate, defines "serapan" loosely as "bekerja/wirausaha/kuliah" (not field-matched) — headline absorption 82.5% likely overstated for field-relevant work. **Conf 4 raw counts, Conf 2 absorption metric.**
6. **Bootcamp self-reported alumni counts (Hacktiv8 3,100+, RevoU 15,000+)** — Marketing round-numbers on official sites, no methodology. RevoU "300k+ users trained" style claims in third-party blogs unverifiable — rejected. **Conf 2.**
7. **Bangkit participant/graduate figures** — mix of official blog (Google Cloud) + participant accounts, mutually consistent. **Conf 3.**
8. **MSIB numbers** — from Kemendikbud socialization decks and official news (Kompas quoting official counts). Batch-by-batch variation in quota vs realized reported inconsistently (13k batch 1, 20k, 25.9k batch 4 etc.) — use quoted official "beyond 150,000 cumulative" with per-batch breakdown where sources identified. **Conf 4.**
9. **University tracer studies** — self-administered surveys, response rates usually 20–40% (response bias likely favorable to employed grads). **Conf 3 individually, Conf 2 aggregated.**
10. **statsindo.com prodi total (34,711)** — aggregator of established PDDikti data. **Conf 3.**

## Data Gaps

1. **Per-year graduate series 2015–2025 by computing major** — not published nationally; requires per-year PDDikti per-prodi queries (each major × each year). Top-20 lists give only recent years for TI and SI.
2. **Exact prodi count for computing cluster** — needs PDDikti program browse query (or LAM InfoKom consolidated accreditation list count).
3. **TKJ and RPL student/graduate counts (SMK)** — data exists in Directorate SMK dashboard filtered by "Teknik Komputer dan Jaringan" and "Pengembangan Perangkat Lunak dan Gim" but values load via JS/API; needs programmatic query (try the public API endpoints behind smk.kemendikdasmen.go.id).
4. **DTS full-year participant realisasi for 2023 and 2024** — Komdigi has a "Rekapitulasi peserta DTS 2023-2024" dataset on data.komdigi.go.id; not yet pulled.
5. **Bootcamp verified numbers (Purwadhika, Impactbyte, Refactory, Skilvul, Salt Academy, BuildWithAngga, Dicoding)** — marketing totals absent or unverifiable; consider LinkedIn alumni counts as alternative methodology.
6. **MSIB technology-field share** — no official stat on proportion of MSIB placements in tech companies vs total; could be approximated from MSIB partner company list (Mitra Pejuang Digital) or Alterra/Bangkit batch quotas.
7. **Computing tracer at national level** (% employed in field, waiting time, first-job salary) — the national Tracer Study (di.banpt / tracerstudy.kemdiktisaintek.go.id) aggregates exist but are not public in tabular form; needs formal data request or use of BAN-PT public dashboards (epsbed successor).
8. **Ministry statements on surplus/shortfall** (e.g., "need 600k digital talents/yr" claims often cited by Kominfo) — were not independently confirmed in this pass; only DTS-related targets confirmed. Mark claim unverified for now.
