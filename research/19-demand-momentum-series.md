## Hiring Events Timeline (big-tech qualitative context)

- **2016–17 (pre-window baseline)**: +60% jump in digital/e-commerce job opportunities; Gojek publicized "200 engineers, 261 million people" scale-up narrative [gojek.io blog :: https://www.gojek.io/blog/200-engineers-261-million-people :: 3]. "Gojek 5,000 engineers target" — **NOT verified** in this session; treat as uncorroborated claim (confidence 2).
- **2018–19**: Bootcamp boom (Hacktiv8, Binar Academy) formed to feed Gojek & peers' tech talent gap [The Ken :: https://the-ken.com/story/hacktiv8-binar-academy-gojek-rush-to-fill-indonesias-tech-talent-gap/ :: 3]. Tech job movers could command +20–35% salary uplift [MP Salary Benchmark 2018 via archive.org :: 20231029195010 :: 4].
- **2020**: COVID freeze; APAC job ads −8–35%; Indonesian hiring conservative, recovering from Q2 [MP TT2021 :: 5].
- **2021**: Boom year — 213 funding rounds/213 deals, new unicorns (ecosystem reached 11 unicorns + 50 centaurs), Gojek–Tokopedia merger forming **GoTo (May 2021)**; MP Index spikes (+62% industrial Q2, +45% fintech Q3 QoQ); 41% headcount-increase intent; fintech/engineering war for talent. Shopee "10,000 hires/year SEA plan" — plausible given Shopee's 2021–22 expansion but **NOT verified** here (confidence 2, uncorroborated).
- **2022**: Great X / churn year — 68% would trade pay for WLB; funding flattens (~$4.2B DS / $3.24B Tracxn from Dec-2022 peak months); H2-2022 funding winter begins (Indonesia.vc: "prolonged funding winter that began in late 2022"). (Mass tech layoffs 2022–23 = sibling agent's layoffs line; Shopee June 2022 layoffs widely documented elsewhere.)
- **2023**: Tech winter — Tracxn $1.3B (−60%); Glints/MHV report tech salary −6%, engineering worst hit; yet 57% of Indonesians job hunting (MP TT2023) and 78% of startups still hiring (Glints); JobStreet: 97% of firms still recruited in 2023.
- **2024**: Crash — Tracxn $323M (−75%); zero new unicorns; NodeFlair/Glints show advertised salary pain延续 (sibling data); JobStreet firms still hiring broadly but IT only 15% of recruitment; MP: 38% orgs struggle to find talent (candidate scarcity persists in tech despite low volume).
- **2025**: Trough with AI-led uptick — YTD equity $167M (−61%); Q3-25 revival deals: Amazon's first major Indonesian startup bet (Astro, $51.9M), AI/fintech rounds (Yup $32M); Korean capital (EV $100M Indonesia-Korea fund); BI easing cycle 150bp since Sep-2024; Glints advertised median recovering (H1'26 +9.1% vs H1'24).
- **2026 (plan)**: Aon tech salary budget 5.9% for 2026; 42% hiring difficulty; MP H2-2025 42% employers expect more hiring → modest demand-side recovery against historically depressed startup funding.

## Methodology Assessment

- Michael Page Talent Trends: employer/employee surveys, 12 APAC markets. 2021: 5,500 businesses/21,000 employees; 2024: 12,940 APAC / 972 ID. Self-selected professional-recruiter audience (white-collar skew; undercounts gig/startup juniors). Intent-to-hire is a forward-looking stated-intent metric, not realized hires.
- MP Employment Index (quarterly): percentage change in job ads on Michael Page Indonesia board — real vacancy flow, professional-tier only.
- Glints × MHV: survey 1,838 employees + C-suite interviews, SG/ID/VN — startup ecosystem specifically, USD medians self-reported; good for startup salary direction, not economy-wide hiring.
- JobStreet (SEEK) outlook reports: 1,162 (2022) / 1,180 (2024) hiring personnel in Indonesia — stated plans, broad-sector.
- DailySocial/DSResearch: manually curated announced equity funding rounds; excludes undisclosed rounds; the $9.44B "peak" (Indonesia.vc) vs $4.3B (DS 2021) gap almost certainly = counting universe (incl. debt, late-stage mega-rounds offshore booking). Tracxn: proprietary tracked-universe extraction, ex-some instruments → systematically lower. For the dashboard: pick ONE series (recommend Tracxn 2022–2025 for shape + DS 2020–2022 for early years, footnoted), or normalize to % of 2021 peak.
- Indonesia.vc: quarterly analyst report; disclosed-equity funding; includes YTD comparisons and debt context.
- LinkedIn LHIR: percentage of members adding new employer, indexed 2016. Indonesia-specific series not retrieved; methodology sound but member-base biased toward formal, white-collar, urban.
