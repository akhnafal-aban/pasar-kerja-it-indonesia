# 12 — MARKET TIGHTNESS: Layoffs Timeline, Hiring Difficulty, Attrition (Indonesia IT Workforce)

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: tightness/froth of Indonesia IT labor market 2022–2026.

## Headline Numbers

1. **Kemnaker (JKP program) PHK totals, all sectors:** 2021: ~127,100 (est.) → 2022: 25,114 → 2023: 64,855 → 2024: 77,965 → 2025: 88,519 → Jan–Oct 2026: 72,336. Cumulative Jan 2023–May 2026: **254,809**. [Kemnaker Satudata via Bisnis.com / Jakarta Globe / detik :: 2022–2026 :: JKP (Unemployment Insurance) claimant counts — covered formal-sector workers who filed claims, NOT total PHK :: https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260114/12/1944213/ri-masih-dilanda-badai-phk-berikut-tren-dalam-5-tahun-terakhir :: CONFIDENCE 4]. Note: this JKP series is much smaller than the separate "237k PHK benefit recipients Jan–Oct 2023" figure found earlier (different program/counting basis — reconciliation needed).
2. **Tech/startup-specific layoffs (crowdsourced tracker ecommurz):** 149 layoff events, ~8,722 people impacted, from 107 unique Indonesian startups since 2020, with peak intensity 2022 (Nov GoTo 1,300) through 2024. [ecommurz.com :: 2020–2024 :: crowdsourced compilation of media-reported layoffs (many "unknown" counts — total is a FLOOR) :: https://ecommurz.com/layoff/ :: CONFIDENCE 3].
3. **Tokopedia post-TikTok-acquisition attrition:** ~2,500 employees at acquisition (early 2024) → repeated cuts June 2024 (~450), Jul–Aug 2025 (~420), 2026 reports that only ~10% remain; TikTok frames 2026 moves as voluntary separation/internal mobility while still hiring ~100 positions. [CNN Indonesia/CNBC Indonesia/bisik.id/Jakarta Globe :: 2024–2026 :: media reports, company partly denies :: https://www.bisik.id/read/tokopedia-kembali-phk-massal-90percent-karyawan-dipangkas-1783122369832 :: CONFIDENCE 3].
4. **Hiring difficulty (ManpowerGroup):** Indonesia is NOT in the 40,413-employer, 42-country 2025 Global Talent Shortage survey sample (verified from survey methodology country list). Best available proxies: APAC 2025 = **77%** of employers struggle to fill roles (India 80%, Singapore 83%, global 74%); APAC IT & Data = **#1 hardest-to-find skill at 32%** of employers; APAC IT industry = 81% scarcity. [ManpowerGroup Talent Shortage Survey 2025 (fieldwork Oct 2024) :: https://www.manpowergroup.com.sg/hubfs/MPG-APAC-Talent-Shortage-2025.pdf :: CONFIDENCE 4 for APAC numbers; Indonesia-specific % = NOT PUBLISHED].
5. **Voluntary attrition (Mercer TRS):** Indonesia overall **8%**; **Emerging Tech (startups) 15%** — highest of all sectors; High Tech 8%. [Mercer Total Remuneration Survey 2022 (550 orgs, Apr–Jun 2022), published Dec 2022 :: https://www.mercer.com/id-id/about/newsroom/companies-in-indonesia-show-steady-recovery/ :: CONFIDENCE 4].
6. **Salary-hopping norm:** Michael Page Indonesia Salary Guide 2025: annual increments **5–8%**, with significant counter-offers in financial services to retain talent (implying external offers materially exceed internal increments). [Michael Page Indonesia Salary Guide 2025 :: https://www.scribd.com/document/832472511/MP-Salary-Guide-2025 :: CONFIDENCE 3].
7. **Hiring intent (JobStreet by SEEK):** 42% of surveyed Indonesian employers predicted MORE active recruitment in H2-2025 vs 2024 (+6pp vs H1-2025 prediction). [JobStreet "Hiring, Compensation, and Benefits 2025" report :: https://markettrack.id/laporan-jobstreet-by-seek-potensi-level-rekrutmen-lebih-aktif-di-paruh-kedua-2025/ :: CONFIDENCE 3].

## Role Distribution of Cuts (evidence points)

- **Gojek 2020 (precursor):** cuts concentrated in GoLife (household services) & GoFood Festivals units, multiple departments [layoffs.fyi :: 2020 :: https://layoffs.fyi/tag/indonesia/ :: CONFIDENCE 4].
- **Tokopedia 2025:** 420 cuts across **IT, customer care, fulfillment, warehouse** — i.e., engineering included, not just ops/marketing [Riaunews/CNBC Indonesia :: Aug 2025 :: https://www.riaunews.com/2025/08/26/tokopedia-phk-ratusan-karyawan-imbas-sengitnya-persaingan-e-commerce/ :: CONFIDENCE 3].
- **Tokopedia 2026:** from ~2,500 at acquisition to ~10% remaining; ~1,100 engineers/tech staff pre-acquisition mostly gone via 3 waves (2024, 2025, 2026) [bogordaily citing internal sources :: Jul 2026 :: https://bogordaily.net/2026/07/phk-karyawan-tokopedia-yang-pergi-bukan-sekadar-pegawai/ :: CONFIDENCE 2].
- **Dropbox 2026 (global pattern):** cuts "especially targeting engineering, product, design, G&A" — engineering no longer protected [Bisnis.com from Yahoo Tech :: 2026 :: CONFIDENCE 3].
- **Populix 2024:** several engineers cut, framed as "performance-based" [murzfeed :: May 2024 :: CONFIDENCE 2].
- **Pattern:** 2022–23 cuts leaned marketing/ops/G&A with engineering partially protected; 2024–26 waves (Tokopedia, Accelbyte 80, Xendit engineering rounds, Populix) hit engineering directly. No comprehensive role-split dataset exists.

## Hiring Difficulty Series

- **ManpowerGroup Global Talent Shortage:** 2021: 54% global / 2022: 75% / 2023: 77% (17-yr high) / 2024: 74–80% (release-dependent) / 2025: 74% global, **APAC 77%** / 2026: "more than 7 in 10" (down modestly). **Indonesia is not in the country sample** (42-country list verified; no Indonesian % for 2021–2025 exists). IT & Data is the #1 hardest-to-find skill in APAC (32%, 2025) and globally (26%, 2025); APAC IT-industry employers 81% struggle. [go.manpowergroup.com/talent-shortage-2023, -2024; MPG-APAC-Talent-Shortage-2025.pdf; manpowergroup.com/en/insights/2026-global-talent-shortage :: 2021–2026 :: employer surveys, n=40,413 (2025) :: CONFIDENCE 4 (APAC/global), N/A Indonesia-specific].
- **JobStreet by SEEK Hiring, Compensation & Benefits 2025:** 42% of Indonesian employers expect more active hiring H2-2025 vs 2024; AI skills, DEI, flexible work as competitive levers. [markettrack.id/industry.co.id/mashable summarizing report :: late 2024/2025 :: employer survey, n unspecified :: CONFIDENCE 3].
- **Michael Page Indonesia Salary Guide 2025:** "measured approach to hiring," cautious optimism under new government; 5–8% increments; counter-offer wars in financial services tech retention. [michaelpage.co.id :: 2025 :: recruiter market view, proprietary :: CONFIDENCE 3].
- **Mercer TRS 2022:** Emerging Tech projected highest 2023 salary increase 8.2% (vs national 6.1%) yet ALSO highest voluntary attrition 15% — tight market for skilled tech workers in 2022; headcount-increasing companies falling 43%→35% (2022→2023), headcount-decreasing 2% (from 5%).
- **Counter-signal (fresh grad):** LPEM FEB UI Labor Market Brief (Jul 2026): S1 fresh graduates face hardest job search on record; CS/IT among degrees with high graduate unemployment — consistent with junior oversupply alongside senior shortage. [Kompas citing LPEM UI :: Aug 2026 :: Sakernas-based :: https://www.kompas.com/tren/read/2026/08/19/120000565/ :: CONFIDENCE 3 (BPS-based; used as directional signal only)].

- No published applicant-per-vacancy or time-to-fill ratios for IT roles in Indonesia were found from Glints/Kalibrr/JobStreet public sources (their reports are gated PDFs). Signals found:
  - Levels.fyi: only **289 self-reported** software-engineer salaries for all Indonesia (244 Jakarta) — tiny pool vs ~450k claimed shortfall; illustrates thinness of the transparent senior-talent market. [levels.fyi :: 2026 :: self-report :: CONFIDENCE 2].
  - JobStreet live counts: 17,010 "fresh graduate" tagged vacancies in Indonesia vs 1,716 labeled all-majors fresh-grad — large nominal demand but concentrated in entry screening. [id.jobstreet.com :: Nov 2026 :: site counts :: CONFIDENCE 2].
  - Michael Page 2025: counter-offers "significant" in financial services → time-to-fill pressure for experienced tech hires. [CONFIDENCE 3].
- **GAP: no credible quantitative time-to-fill series for Indonesia IT.** News datapoints of "1 lowongan X pelamar" not verified in this pass.

## Attrition

- **Mercer TRS 2022 (Dec 2022):** Indonesia voluntary attrition 8% overall; **Emerging Tech 15%**, Consumer Goods 9%, High Tech 8%. Cause: more opportunities for skilled professionals + workers fleeing unstable startups post-layoffs ("leaving Emerging Tech... for more stability"). [Mercer :: 550 organizations, 7 industries :: https://www.mercer.com/id-id/about/newsroom/companies-in-indonesia-show-steady-recovery/ :: CONFIDENCE 4]. No newer (2023–2026) Mercer Indonesia TRS press figure found in this pass.
- **Populix Working Trend Feb 2023:** Gen Z considers 1–2 years the ideal tenure before switching; millennials 5+ years — behavioral norm of job-hopping among junior tech workers. [Populix via espos.id :: 2023 :: online survey :: CONFIDENCE 2].
- **Salary-hopping:** Mercer 2022: external market rewards exceeded internal increments (Emerging Tech salary-increase projection cut for 2023 as investment slowed); Michael Page 2025: 5–8% increments + financial-services counter-offer wars → moving jobs remained the primary raise mechanism. Specific "20–30% jump" figures circulating in blogs were NOT verified against a primary source. [GAP].

## Kemnaker PHK Series (official, JKP claimant basis)

- Yearly: 2021 ~127,100 (back-estimated by Bisnis from −80.24% YoY) / 2022 25,114 / 2023 64,855 / **2024 77,965** / **2025 88,519** / 2026 Jan–May 23,470, Jan–Oct **72,336**. [Kemnaker Satudata / Pusdatin via Bisnis.com, detikFinance, Jakarta Globe, satudata.kemnaker.go.id :: JKP benefit claimants :: CONFIDENCE 4].
- 2025 intra-year: Jan–Jun 42,385 (+32.19% YoY, Central Java highest mid-year); Jan–Aug 44,333. [detikFinance/Bisnis :: 2025 :: CONFIDENCE 4].
- Province splits published: 2025 West Java 21.26% of total; 2026 Jan–May West Java 5,044 (21.5%); 2024 DKI Jakarta largest (kumparan recap: ~80,000 workers at 60companies — figure differs from JKP count, likely includes non-JKP claims). [CONFIDENCE 3].
- **These are all-sector figures.** Tech share not isolated by Kemnaker in public dashboards. Media-documented individual tech events (ecommurz) sum to ~10–15k 2022–2025 — roughly 2–4% of all-sector PHK in 2023–24, but tech layoffs are under-represented in JKP claims because startup severance is often paid privately without claims.
- Official PHK notification data (SIAP Kerja/Sisapat): no published aggregate retrieved; satudata dataset "Tenaga Kerja Ter-PHK" is the operative official series.

## Methodology Assessment

- **Kemnaker JKP series (best official series):** counts workers who FILED Unemployment Insurance claims → lower bound for formal-sector PHK; excludes informal, private-severance, and non-enrolled workers. Tech workers at well-paying startups often don't claim → tech layoffs undercounted here. Consistent definition 2022–2026 makes trend valid.
- **ecommurz/layoffs.fyi:** crowdsourced from local media + anonymous submissions ("murzfeed"); many events have unknown headcount → totals are floors; double-counting risk (e.g., repeated "silent layoff" mentions of same company quarters apart). layoffs.fyi Indonesia tag covers only 2020 era (Gojek 430, Sorabel shutdown).
- **ManpowerGroup:** Indonesia not sampled in Talent Shortage 2021–2025 — any "Indonesia %" circulating in press is actually APAC/global misattribution (verified: Indonesian press stories quote the 77% APAC number as regional). Use APAC + Singapore/India as anchors.
- **Mercer TRS:** proper sample (550 orgs) but 2022 vintage; tech attrition likely softened 2023–2024 (layoff wave) — treat 15% Emerging Tech attrition as the 2022 peak-froth datapoint.
- **BPS (banned unless methodology described):** avoided except one directional fresh-grad cite (LPEM Sakernas-based), clearly labeled.

## Data Gaps

1. **No Indonesia-specific ManpowerGroup Talent Shortage % exists for 2021–2025** — Indonesia absent from the 40k-employer sample. Indonesian media quoting "77%" are citing APAC.
2. **Applicant-per-vacancy and time-to-fill for IT roles:** Glints/Kalibrr/JobStreet don't publish these publicly; needs gated report access or primary interviews.
3. **Attrition 2023–2026:** only Mercer 2022 vintage available in open press; later TRS waves behind paywall. LinkedIn Global Talent Trends Indonesia slices not retreived.
4. **Percent salary jump when switching jobs:** blog claims of 20–30% unverified against primary source; only directional evidence (5–8% internal increments vs counter-offer wars).
5. **Tech-sector share of Kemnaker PHK:** Satudata has province but no IT-sector split in public datasets; the two series (JKP claimants 64.9k vs "237k recipients Jan–Oct 2023" from PHK program) must be reconciled — different program definitions.
6. **Precise 2024–2025 tech layoff totals:** ecommurz coverage thins after mid-2024 (moved to murzfeed), many rounds "unknown" — 2024+ tech-specific totals known only for named events.
7. **Shopee/Xendit role composition:** media say "multiple departments"/"several engineers" without percentages.
