# Indonesia IT Services & Outsourcing Sector — Market Size + Formal Employment

Date compiled: 2026-11-09. All figures with lineage. Marketing-firm estimates capped at Conf 3 per method rules.

## Headline Numbers

- Indonesia IT services market (spend on externally provided IT/BPO services): **USD 4.76–6.3 bn in 2024–2025** depending on source; fastest credible anchor: Mordor **USD 4.76 bn (2025) → USD 5.41 bn (2026) → USD 9.52 bn (2031), CAGR ~12%**.
- BPO sub-market: **USD ~1.93 bn (2024) → USD 3.56 bn (2029), CAGR 10.2%**.
- ICT services exports (BoP, IMF/WB): **USD 3.76 bn in 2024**, ~9.6% of Indonesia's total services exports.
- Largest IT-services employers (prior agent data reused, not re-researched): Telkomsigma ~5,300–6,000; Metrodata group ~4,077; Accenture ID 1,359; IBM Consulting ID >1,000; Astragraphia (IT services division).
- Vendor-placement model evidenced at banks: BCA had **>1,400 internal IT staff mid-2022** and targeted 2,000 more IT hires by 2023 — vendor (alih daya) staffing is standard complement; Bank Mandiri historically ran **~18,000 outsourcing workers vs 46,000 total staff** (all functions, not IT-only; old figure, use as order-of-magnitude only).

## Vendor Placement Scale ("rental" IT staff / body shopping)

- BCA: >1,400 IT staff as of June 2022, plan to hire 2,000 more IT workers through 2023 — confirms large in-house IT; BCA EVP publicly said outsourcing stigma hurt hiring, implying heavy vendor-worker use [Liputan6 :: 2022 :: executive statement :: https://www.liputan6.com/bisnis/read/5037765/bca-butuh-2000-tenaga-it-hingga-2023-siapa-minat :: Conf 3].
- BCA total staff 27,707 (Sep 30, 2026), down 0.82% y/y [stockanalysis.com :: 2026 :: from filings :: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/idx/BBCA/employees/ :: Conf 4].
- Bank Mandiri: of ~46,000 total staff, ~18,000 were outsourcing workers (with ~7,900 named permanent at that time) — all functions, not IT-specific; article dates from Manpower Law debate era (~2012–2013); treat as historical order-of-magnitude only [BeritaSatu :: ~2012 :: executive/media statement :: https://www.beritasatu.com/ekonomi/27498/bank-mandiri-tidak-terkendala-aturan-alih-daya :: Conf 2].
- Indonesian outsourcing/manpower associations (Abadi — Asosiasi Penyedia Jasa Alih Daya; with Apindo) formally opposed total abolition of outsourcing in the 2026 draft Labour Law (RUU Ketenagakerjaan), arguing it would cause mass layoffs and raise informality — confirms the placement model is large and politically contested [Kompas :: 2026 :: association statement :: https://money.kompas.com/read/2026/10/01/050600826/ :: Conf 3].
- No published vendor-side headcount of IT-specific alih daya inside banks/telcos, and no published rate cards from outsourcing firms found in English/Indonesian free sources (see Data Gaps).

## ICT Services Export

- **ICT service exports (BoP, current USD): USD 3,760,052,545 in 2024** [World Bank WDI indicator BX.GSR.CCIS.CD, source IMF Balance of Payments Statistics Yearbook — includes computer & communications (telecom, postal/courier) and information services; retrieved via TradingEconomics Nov 2026 :: https://tradingeconomics.com/indonesia/ict-service-exports-bop-us-dollar-wb-data.html :: Conf 4 — official BoP lineage, IMF-compiled, independent of BPS tables].
- ICT service exports = **9.62% of Indonesia's total services exports**, below all-time high 13.53% (2007) [economicsapi.com citing same WB series :: 2025 :: Conf 3].
- Trend: series rose strongly post-2020 with the digital boom; 2024 = 3.76 bn implies roughly quadrupling from ~1 bn levels of mid-2010s (chart visible on TE page, exact annual values behind interactive element — flagged as partial gap).

## Methodology Assessment

- All market-size figures are **lead-gen marketing estimates** from research firms (Mordor, Ken, VMR, SkyQuest, GlobalData). Their internal methods are described (Mordor's is the most explicit: 9-months-actuals+Q4 projection base year, triangulated against provider revenues and contract awards; Statista: key-player financials + associations + GDP/internet regressions) but none publish reproducible calculations. Two-plus-x spread between low (4.76 bn) and high (12.5 bn contract-inclusive) definitions. Confidence 3 ceiling applied.
- Employment: reliable only at firm level via annual reports/filings (prior agent) and stockanalysis.com filing-derived counts (Conf 4). Sector-level employment has NO usable association figure — the often-cited "millions of IT workers" numbers are demand projections (Kemnaker) or blog aggregations (Conf 1).
- Vendor placement: only fragmented evidence; outsourcing-worker counts inside banks are old and not IT-specific. The 2026 RUU Ketenagakerjaan debate confirms scale is politically significant but unquantified publicly.
- ICT export figure is the most rigorous number in this file (IMF BoP lineage via WB WDI).

## Data Gaps

1. **No verified Indonesian IT-services industry association employment figure.** Could not confirm a dedicated association (candidates like "APJI"/"MITRA" do not exist as IT-services employer bodies in found sources; APJII is ISPs, Komdigi is government). Sector formal employment must be built bottom-up from company filings.
2. **Statista Indonesia IT-services values paywalled** — only structure visible (IT Outsourcing = largest segment). A paid pull would give the full 4-segment series 2011–2031.
3. **Vendor-placement (body shopping) IT headcounts inside banks/telcos not published** — only a stale all-function Mandiri figure (2012) and qualitative evidence. Rate cards not public.
4. **Indonesia's exact 2023 GSLI rank not retrievable** free; 2023 press coverage focuses India/China/Malaysia + APAC risers.
5. **Annual ICT export time series values** (beyond 2024 endpoint and 9.62% share) behind interactive charts; WB API pull would fill 2020–2023.
6. **Sub-segment values for SI / managed services / IT consulting** individually paywalled everywhere; only shares and qualitative ranking available free.
7. IMARC/others' "Indonesia ICT market" figures (50.2 bn 2025) inconsistent with GlobalData (41.99 bn 2023) — definitional scope (includes telecom services revenue); do not mix with IT-services-only series.
