## Enterprise DX & Adoption
- **IDC APAC cloud survey (Oct 2024, N=784 APAC enterprises):** ~90% of APAC enterprises have multi-cloud workloads; **lack of cloud skills** and delivery slippage = top challenges → direct IT hiring pressure. [IDC PR :: 2024-10-21 :: IT & Business Services Sourcing Survey 2H24 :: CONF 4; APAC incl Indonesia, no local n]
- **IDC Indonesia Enterprise Survey 2022:** hybrid cloud = top adoption approach next 12–18 months for 32.7% of Indonesian enterprises (public 22.8%, private 25.7%); financial services prefers private cloud 42.9%; ~90% of Indonesian media companies already use cloud. [IDC :: 2022 :: enterprise survey :: via Huawei white paper :: CONF 3]
- **Mekari/Litbang Kompas survey (2024):** 65% of large/mid-size urban companies say DX raises effectiveness; **73% of non-users plan to adopt cloud software within 2 years**; 55% have used cloud software >3 years. [Mekari Conference research :: 2024-09 :: company survey :: https://voi.id/en/economy/416785 :: CONF 3]
- **Alibaba Cloud/NielsenIQ (survey Sep–Oct 2022, 1,000 Asian decision-makers):** 94% of Indonesian cloud users planned to increase cloud investment in 2023 (highest in Asia); 92% planned complete migration within 2 years; 53% expected full migration within 6 months. [NielsenIQ for Alibaba Cloud :: 2022 :: vendor-commissioned survey, Dec 2022 whitepaper + VOI :: CONF 3 — legacy data, actuals unknown]
- **ADBI (2024):** Indonesian enterprise cloud adoption **<30%** — lowest tier in Asia-Pacific together with India/Vietnam/Philippines → large headroom = forward IT hiring demand. [ADBI Policy Brief :: 2024 :: national stats + modeled estimates :: CONF 3]
- **SAP ECC→S/4HANA cycle:** mainstream maintenance of ECC 6.0 (EhP6–8) ends **31 Dec 2027**; ~35,000 ECC customers globally, ~39% had S/4 licenses by end-2024; clearing backlog would need ~75% more SAP capacity than market holds (Basis Technologies); S/4HANA consultant fees +~20% since 2023 (Baer Group). **Indonesia-specific commentary not found** — large Indonesian enterprises (SOEs, banks, telcos, manufacturing) run SAP heavily; mid-size Indonesian core-banking/system-replacement deals not aggregated anywhere public. [multiple secondary/consultancy sources 2026 :: CONF 3 as global backdrop, 2 for Indonesia spillover]
- **PDP Law compliance (Oct 2024 deadline)** cited as a spending trigger for cybersecurity/customer-platform investment in 2025 (Ken Research). CONF 3.

## Government IT Spend (outside BPS)
- APBN 2025: total national spending Rp3,621.3T; K/L (ministry) spending Rp1,160.1T; **no aggregated IT/SPBE line item published publicly in budget documents**. Kemenkeu requires SPBE clearance (KemenPANRB/Bappenas/Komdigi) for IT application/data center/app spending covering only: new app systems, new servers/DCs, special-purpose systems/pemrosesan data — clearance process exists, but total TI procurement value not aggregated publicly anywhere.
- Indications instead: Inpres 1/2025 efficiency cut Rp256.1T from K/L budgets → 2025 government IT spending faced a dampener (IT vendors such as Metrodata called out "weakened government spending" hurting ICT sector 2025). [Metrodata Electronics AR 2025 :: official listed-company filing :: https://idx.co.id/StaticData/NewsAndAnnouncement/ANNOUNCEMENTSTOCK/From_EREP/202604/0e2a3291d2_4ddee47914.pdf :: CONF 4]
- LPSE ([e-procurement]) TI category totals: no published aggregate found. GAP.
- ICT distributor evidence: Metrodata (largest listed ICT distributor) sales Rp27.2T (2025) → 27.8T (18.5T 2021) — bottom-up confirmation ICT demand growth minus softer 2025 public sector.

## Methodology Assessment
- Gartner/IDC PRs: vendor sales-analysis methodology described in Gartner PRs (1,000+ vendor sales, quarterly databook) — audit-level visibility into Indonesia only via paid databook; public PR for Indonesia nonexistent 2023-26, so treat any "Indonesia IT spending = $XXB (Gartner)" claim on the open web as UNSOURCED unless it links a Gartner document.
- Ken Research/Credence/6Wresearch market reports: syndicated models, no published methodology; suggest cross-checking the DX number (~$24B) against IDC Indonesia's published $1B public cloud figure — implies the $24B covers broad DX incl services/hardware, not just software. Read as order-of-magnitude.
- Hiring surveys (Aon/Robert Walters/Michael Page/PwC): real surveys, Indonesia Ns modest (400–972 per market); measure sentiment/percentages, not headcount counts — cannot be added into a jobs-stock model, only used as directional hiring indicators.
- Hyperscaler "jobs" numbers (AWS econ model 24,700 job-years; Microsoft 840K trained; Indosat 1M trained): "trained" tickets ≠ IT employment; econ-model jobs are modeled, not payroll. Verify before using as stock/flow in workforce model.
- SAP 2027: global deadline framing, no Indonesia-specific migration or consultant counts published — do not overstate.

## Data Gaps
1. **No Gartner Indonesia IT spending press release exists 2023–2026** — any public broad "Indonesia IT spend $XXB" claim should be traced or dropped. Country slices live in paywalled databook only.
2. **IDC Indonesia ICT spending dollar total** not public; only public cloud subtotal and ASEAN aggregates are open.
3. **Google, Alibaba, Apple data center investments in Indonesia: no verifiable primary announcement found 2024–2026** — do not cite $2B Google or Apple Bali DC until verified.
4. Government IT spend: only clearance process exists (SPBE) — no aggregated rp TI procurement number found in Kemenkeu/LKPP/Komdigi sources; LPSE TI totals not published.
5. IT headcount expansion % for Indonesian non-tech firms (Michael Page/Robert Walters) is gated; only qualitative statements confirm tech hiring remains the in-demand area alongside 20-30% premium treatment of top candidates.
6. Bank IT budgets for BNI/BRI/Mandiri/BTN are director statements, not breakouts; BCA's actual TI spend line and ~5,000 mid/small bank items unaccounted.
7. SAP ECC 2027 Indonesia-specific effect (migration counts, consultant shortfall, mid-market go-lives) not quantified publicly.
8. SOE IT budgets outside banks/telcos invisible in public data.
