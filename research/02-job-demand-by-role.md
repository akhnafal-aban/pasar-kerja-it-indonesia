## Methodology Assessment

- **LinkedIn guest counts (primary demand metric):** live, deduplicated country postings matched by keyword. Strengths: current, reproducible, per-role. Weaknesses: (1) coverage bias — LinkedIn over-indexes white-collar/tech vs JobStreet/Karir/Glints blue-collar-heavy mix; (2) keyword overlap ("programmer" vs "software engineer" vs "software developer" are largely the same postings), so counts must not be summed; (3) location=Indonesia misses fully-remote-global roles hiring Indonesians; (4) no seniority split. Not total market size.
- **Michael Page:** averages from ~5,000 benchmark points (placements/client input). Skews to professional roles placed via recruiters (MNC/large local), i.e. upper band vs market.
- **Robert Walters:** range per role from recruiter desk knowledge; per-annual figures exclude bonuses; tech & transformation only.
- **Glints:** largest posting-level dataset (SME/startup heavy, junior-skewed); advertised ≠ paid salary; salary-disclosure posting subset (15K/semester tech) is a minority of postings (selection bias toward transparent/smaller employers).
- **LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise:** growth in member titles 2022-01→2024-07, excludes internships, roles dominated by few employers; rank not percentage; 3-yr lookback shortened from 5 yrs in 2025 edition (limits cross-year comparability).
- **BPS avoided** per rules. Talent-gap figures (9M / 3–6M by 2030) are planning projections with unpublished methodology — flagged low confidence.
